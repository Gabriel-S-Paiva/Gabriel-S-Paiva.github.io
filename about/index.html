Hi, I'm Gabriel.

I'm a third-year Web Information Systems and Technologies student at ESMAD, focused on backend systems, REST APIs and self-hosted infrastructure.

I came to web development through the advanced web design and programming course at MasterD (2023-2025, graded 20/20), then moved on to ESMAD. I started on Node, Express and Vue, and picked up Go and SvelteKit while building Owned Cloud.

What I enjoy most is owning a system end to end: the schema, the API, the auth model, the deployment and the CI that ships it. Owned Cloud, my self-hosted family cloud storage, runs on a repurposed laptop at home with Docker Compose and Caddy, and so does this site.

I also like to compete. I have four first places from hackathons, project awards and a programming contest, and I was an invited speaker at an ESMAD seminar on academic success in group work.

Before software I trained as a professional musician (wind and percussion, 2020-2023), and I have been playing since 2015.
